    How it works

    From a sentence or a photo to the right product

    1. Words and photos become vectorsCLIP maps text and images to 512 numbers in one shared space, so “red sneaker” and a photo of one land close together.
    2. Fine-tuned on Amazon listingsContrastive training on 109,395 product photo–title pairs from Amazon Berkeley Objects taught it how products are really described.
    3. A compressed index finds matchesEvery catalog photo is pre-embedded into a FAISS IVF-PQ index of about 7 MB, scanned in milliseconds.
    4. Everything runs on your deviceINT8-quantized ONNX encoders run in WebAssembly. The same model is also available as a Dockerised FastAPI service.
